# ATLAS Smart Money Concepts

## Purpose and source

This skill recovers the XAU Cartel Order Blocks and Fair Value Gaps lessons from:
- `ATLAS_Complete_Market_Reading_Learning_Guide.pdf`
- `ATLAS_Market_Reading_Notes.pdf`

These concepts are used as explanatory frameworks for price delivery. They do not prove who traded at a candle, guarantee a reaction, or form a validated trading system by themselves.

## 1. Start with structure and liquidity context

Before labelling a zone, establish:
- Higher-timeframe trend or range.
- Meaningful swing levels and relevant BOS/CHoCH.
- The nearby liquidity pool price interacted with or may be targeting.
- Whether the move is genuine displacement or ordinary volatility/chop.

Use `atlas-market-structure` and `atlas-liquidity` where those skills are available. Do not treat an isolated candle pattern as sufficient context.

## 2. Displacement

Displacement is a strong directional move showing forceful price delivery. Assess whether the move:
- Left an obvious area with decisive movement.
- Closed beyond a meaningful structural level.
- Followed a relevant liquidity event.
- Left an imbalance/FVG.
- Aligns with higher-timeframe context or a clearly explained transition.

A large candle alone is not automatically meaningful displacement; assess the surrounding structure and market conditions.

## 3. Candidate Order Blocks (OB)

The source lessons define a candidate OB as the last opposing candle before a strong displacement:
- **Bullish OB candidate:** the last bearish candle before a strong rally.
- **Bearish OB candidate:** the last bullish candle before a strong decline.

The candle's high-to-low range is often marked as a potential reaction zone. Assess:
- Was there meaningful liquidity context before the move?
- Did price leave the zone with clear displacement?
- Did the move break a relevant swing level?
- Did the move leave an FVG/imbalance?
- Is the zone fresh, partially mitigated or repeatedly revisited?
- Does it align with higher-timeframe context and a meaningful liquidity objective?

A **fresh** candidate has not yet been revisited after displacement. A **mitigated** zone has been revisited/traded into. Repeated visits may reduce its usefulness because the context and resting interest may have changed.

A return to the zone is an area to study for rejection or continuation, not an automatic entry. The candle does not prove that a bank or institution placed orders there, and the zone is not guaranteed to hold.

## 4. Fair Value Gaps (FVG)

An FVG is a three-candle non-overlap associated with rapid price delivery, where the first and third candles' wicks do not overlap and the middle candle commonly provides displacement.

- **Bullish FVG:** candle 3 low is above candle 1 high. The gap is between candle 1 high and candle 3 low.
- **Bearish FVG:** candle 3 high is below candle 1 low. The gap is between candle 3 high and candle 1 low.
- **Consequent Encroachment (CE):** the 50% midpoint of the FVG; sometimes watched as a potential reaction area.
- **Inverse FVG (IFVG):** a gap decisively closed through that may later be watched for a role reversal.

Assess:
- Clear displacement versus small gaps in choppy conditions.
- Whether the move broke meaningful structure.
- Whether a liquidity event preceded the displacement.
- Higher-timeframe alignment or a clearly explained reversal context.
- Freshness, partial fill, full mitigation or decisive close through the zone.
- Confluence with a candidate OB or structural level.

Price may partially fill, reach CE, fully fill, or never revisit an FVG. Never promise a fill. A decisive close through the zone can weaken its original interpretation; an IFVG role reversal must be supported by subsequent price behaviour.

## 5. Integrate the concepts as one price-delivery narrative

A useful sequence from the lessons is:

Higher-timeframe context → obvious liquidity pool → possible sweep or breakout → structure response → displacement → candidate OB/FVG → retracement and observed reaction → next opposing liquidity objective.

For a reported setup or full analysis, explain which links in this sequence are actually present and which are missing. Consider the alternative that price is breaking out, continuing, or simply moving through ordinary volatility.

Do not count the same candle's wick, FVG and displacement label as three independent pieces of confluence. Confluence is stronger when reasonably independent evidence aligns across structure, liquidity, delivery and timeframe.

## Output discipline

When presenting an OB/FVG candidate, state its price boundaries only from actual chart data, timeframe, context, freshness/mitigation and invalidation. Separate:
- Observation: what the candles did.
- Interpretation: why a zone may matter.
- Scenario: what a return or continuation could do.
- Confirmation/invalidation: what would change the view.

Do not reuse historical example levels from the supplied videos as current levels. This skill teaches analysis only; it does not issue automatic entries or authorise trades.
