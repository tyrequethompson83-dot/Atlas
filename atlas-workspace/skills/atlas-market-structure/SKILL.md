# ATLAS Market Structure

## Purpose and source

This skill recovers the Market Structure lesson from the supplied XAU Cartel video material, consolidated in:

## 1. Establish meaningful swings

Focus on significant swing highs and swing lows, not every candle fluctuation.

- **Uptrend:** a meaningful sequence of higher highs (HH) and higher lows (HL).
- **Downtrend:** a meaningful sequence of lower highs (LH) and lower lows (LL).
- **Range:** repeated tests around relatively stable boundaries, often with similar highs/lows and no sustained directional structure.

A lower high or higher low is meaningful only in relation to the previous relevant swing and the timeframe being analysed. Do not promote every small internal fluctuation to major structure.

## 2. Read BOS and CHoCH from closes

- **BOS (Break of Structure):** price closes beyond a meaningful swing in the direction of the prevailing structure. A decisive close is stronger evidence than a wick that briefly pierces the level and closes back inside.
- **CHoCH (Change of Character):** a meaningful break against the current swing sequence that warns the structure may be shifting. It is an early warning, not proof that a full reversal has begun.
- **Pullback:** countertrend movement that can remain corrective while the relevant higher-timeframe swing structure holds.

In an uptrend, a decisive close below the last meaningful HL can warn that bullish structure is weakening. In a downtrend, a decisive close above the last meaningful LH can warn of a potential bullish transition. Look for follow-through and retests before describing a confirmed larger change.

## 3. Use multiple timeframes coherently

1. Start with 4H or 1H to establish broad trend/range, major swings and decision levels.
2. Use 15M or 5M to inspect the reaction only when price approaches an area that matters.
3. A bullish lower-timeframe sequence may be a pullback inside a bearish higher-timeframe structure.
4. If timeframes disagree, state the conflict and explain which level would resolve it. Do not force agreement.
5. Do not treat each lower-timeframe break as a major BOS or CHoCH.

Use the data actually available. State the instrument, source, timeframe and timestamps. Do not infer the active chart if the runtime does not supply trusted chart context.

## 4. Structure analysis sequence

For a full read:
- Identify the current environment: trend, range, transition or unclear.
- Mark the last meaningful high and low that define the current sequence.
- Describe HH/HL or LH/LL progression and the relevant structural boundaries.
- Identify whether price has closed beyond a meaningful swing.
- Determine whether the move supports continuation, a potential shift, or remains a pullback.
- Explain the higher-timeframe/lower-timeframe relationship.
- State the evidence that would strengthen or invalidate the current interpretation.

## 5. Connect structure to the other skills

Structure supplies context; it does not independently explain every move.

- Use `atlas-liquidity` when obvious highs/lows and likely stop clusters matter.
- Use `atlas-smart-money-concepts` when displacement, OBs or FVGs add explanatory value.
- Use `atlas-news-impact` or `atlas-macro-research` when a current catalyst or scheduled release may explain repricing.
- Use `atlas-trade-scenario-analysis` to combine evidence into conditional outcomes.

The connected narrative taught by the XAU Cartel material is higher-timeframe context → liquidity interaction → structure response → displacement → possible retracement zone → next opposing objective. Do not force every stage when the evidence is absent.

## Analytical boundaries

- A wick is not automatically a BOS.
- A CHoCH is not automatically a reversal.
- A lower-timeframe trend does not automatically override a higher-timeframe trend.
- Do not reuse historical example levels from the supplied videos.
- Separate chart observation from interpretation and scenario.
- No numerical win probability unless a calibrated model and relevant data exist.

This skill teaches analysis only. It does not create signals or authorise trading.
